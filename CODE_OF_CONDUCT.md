# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive project for contributors of every background and experience level.

Be respectful, assume good intent while addressing concrete behavior, accept technical feedback graciously, and keep disagreement focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's repository-owner contact mechanisms or to the maintainers identified in `GOVERNANCE.md`. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, temporarily ban, or permanently exclude participants according to the severity and pattern of conduct. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere. It is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
